Runs entirely in your browser — files never leave your device.
Best-effort layout preservation: exact text, fonts & structure are kept; pixel-identical positioning is not something any browser-based converter can guarantee.

Drop a PDF here or click to browse
.pdf up to ~30MB
How it works: each page's text is read with its exact position, font size and bold/italic styling via pdf.js, then rebuilt as real, editable Word paragraphs (not an image). Multi-column layouts, scanned/image-only PDFs, and complex tables are the main cases where spacing may need manual touch-up afterward.
📝
Drop a .docx here or click to browse
.docx up to ~30MB
How it works: the .docx is parsed into structured HTML (headings, bold/italic, lists, tables) via mammoth.js, laid out on true A4 pages, then rasterized page-by-page into a PDF. Custom fonts embedded in the .docx aren't extracted — the closest web-safe equivalent is used instead.
Built with pdf.js, mammoth.js, jsPDF & html2canvas — all processing is local to this page.
